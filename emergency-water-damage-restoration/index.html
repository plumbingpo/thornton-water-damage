When to Call for Emergency Water Damage Restoration in Thornton

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Water is coming through a ceiling, light fixture or electrical outlet.
  • A growing pool on the floor is heading for the next room or running downstairs.
  • Walls on the lower floor are wet from top to bottom because of a leak overhead.
  • Trapped water overhead has made part of the ceiling bulge or sag.
  • You have no way to locate or get to the shutoff while water continues to pour.
  • What is on the floor came up from the sewer, or it is discolored and smells foul.
  • A water heater tank has failed and is emptying across the floor.

How to Prevent Emergency Water Damage Restoration Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Test your sump pump before the rainy season and consider a battery backup.
  • Put leak alarms beneath sinks and beside the dishwasher, washer and water heater.
  • Store valuables and important papers off the floor, especially in basements and lower levels.
  • Wrap plumbing in cold, unheated parts of the house so a hard freeze cannot crack it.
  • Being able to find the electrical panel quickly means wet areas can be de-energized without risk.
  • Keep the number of an emergency restoration company saved in your phone before you need it.
