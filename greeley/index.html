Restoration Challenges Greeley Homeowners Face

Most water damage calls we take in Greeley trace back to a short list of causes. Knowing them makes it easier to catch trouble while it is still a small repair.

Pipes: When water freezes inside a line it can crack the metal or plastic, then pour out as soon as things warm up. Depending on the season, the plumbing most at risk runs through outside walls, attics and garages without heat. In older homes, aging joints and tired connectors sometimes fail with no cold snap involved. Shut off the main valve right away, because an open line can soak several rooms quickly.

Appliances: The thin hoses feeding a washer, dishwasher or ice maker do not last forever. Tanks rust from the inside, so an old water heater may drip for weeks or give out suddenly. These failures do the most harm when the appliance is on an upper floor or above finished living space.

Overflows: A clogged toilet or a sink left running can put water on the floor in minutes. From there it slips beneath the trim and can drip into whatever sits under that floor. Water that backed up out of a drain or toilet bowl is not clean, so keep kids and pets out of it.

Roof: Missing shingles, cracked flashing and clogged gutters let rain into the attic. By the time a brown ring appears overhead, the attic insulation has often been wet for a while. The crew from Thornton Restoration can dry the attic and ceiling, but the roof itself needs a repair to stop the leak.

Basement: Once the ground outside is soaked, water finds its way into a basement through wall cracks and the seam where floor meets wall. If the sump pump sticks or the electricity goes out mid-storm, the pit fills and spills across the slab. Finished basements suffer most, since carpet, drywall and framing all soak up water from the bottom.

Mold: Damp drywall, wood and carpet can begin to grow mold in as little as 24 to 48 hours. A lot of the mold we find in Greeley comes from a past leak that looked dry on the surface only. A musty smell, peeling paint or dark spotting near a previous repair is worth having checked.

The First Hour After Water Damage in Greeley

The first hour is when good intentions cause the most trouble. These are the mistakes our crews see most often in Greeley, and what to do instead.

Do not use a household vacuum on water. It is an electric shock risk and it will ruin the machine; use towels and a mop. Do not turn on ceiling fans or lights under a wet ceiling, and stay out of rooms where the ceiling is sagging. Leave fitted carpet in place, because pulling it up the wrong way can stretch or rip a carpet we might have saved. Avoid cranking up the heating, as warmth plus moisture encourages mold.

Do shut off the water, open cabinet doors and closets so air can reach them, and hang up wet fabrics and cushions to drip. Photograph each room before you shift a single item. Do keep children and pets away from the area. Most of all, phone early, so a Thornton Restoration crew is already heading for Greeley while you work through this list.
