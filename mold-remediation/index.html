How to Prevent Mold Remediation Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Clean and maintain your HVAC system and change filters regularly to prevent mold in ductwork
  • Use mold-resistant drywall and paint in moisture-prone areas like bathrooms and basements
  • Keep indoor humidity below 60% (ideally 30 to 50%) with dehumidifiers and proper ventilation
  • Address condensation on windows, pipes and cold surfaces promptly
  • Keep gutters clean so rainwater does not seep in along the foundation
  • Make sure bathrooms, kitchens and laundry areas have adequate exhaust ventilation

Signs You Need Mold Remediation in Thornton

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Belongings in storage, including clothes and cardboard, showing spots or fuzz
  • Recurring respiratory symptoms, allergies or headaches that improve when you are away from home
  • Cleaning that never lasts, with growth returning to the same surface
  • An earthy, musty smell that will not go away, mostly in humid or closed-off spaces
  • Stains spreading on a ceiling or wall, or dark marks ringing the air vents
  • Windows, cold surfaces or walls that sweat with condensation on a regular basis
  • A slow leak under a sink, behind an appliance or in the roof that went unnoticed

Why Mold Remediation Is Not a DIY Job

Mold remediation is not a DIY project. Disturbing mold colonies without proper containment sends millions of spores airborne, which can contaminate unaffected areas of your home and create worse health exposure than the original problem. Capturing and containing the spores during removal takes HEPA-filtered scrubbers, protective equipment for the crew and containment kept under negative air pressure, which is how professionals do it.

A professional job goes after the moisture source too, because that is why the mold grew in the first place. If the moisture is left alone, taking away what you can see only buys time, and regrowth often shows up within weeks. Finding concealed moisture, drying it properly and preventing a repeat all take equipment and training, which certified remediation technicians have.
